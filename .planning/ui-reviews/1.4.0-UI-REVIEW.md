## Top 3 non-blocking follow-ups

1. **Reduce 390px header density** — The controls remain usable but visually compact — collapse one secondary metadata element or move it below the title at the narrowest breakpoint.
2. **Soften the mobile reader Close treatment** — Close is non-destructive but currently uses rose emphasis — use the neutral secondary-action palette while retaining its 44px target and accessible label.
3. **Add a narrow Chinese reader capture** — Chinese strings are present but the final screenshot matrix covers Korean and English — add one 390px expanded-reader state to lock down label width and wrapping.

## Detailed findings

### 1. Copywriting — 9.98/10

- Conversation actions use short, direct localized verbs: Send, Stop, Collapse, Copy, Close, and New AI session. The expanded-reader renderer supplies both full and narrow labels (`renderer/app-drawer-content.js:93-118`).
- Session-reset copy explicitly says that conversation history remains while active context starts over (`renderer/i18n-messages.js:1621-1629`). The confirmation capture makes Cancel and Start new session unambiguous.
- “New AI session” accurately describes a fresh context without implying that history is deleted. The drawer button also exposes a descriptive accessible label and help title (`renderer/app-drawer.js:210-211`).
- Korean and English expanded-reader captures both retain concise labels without truncation. Chinese strings exist for the same reset and reader contracts; a dedicated narrow Chinese capture would be useful additional coverage, not a blocker.

### 2. Visual hierarchy — 9.92/10

- Major views retain a clear page heading, primary work surface, and restrained secondary metadata. Provider and status colors support scanning without competing with the focal content.
- Conversation bubbles, prompt preview, message metadata, and composer are visually separated. The composer input surface and action rail read as distinct regions.
- Stop response remains the sole destructive red action; New AI session now uses neutral slate/cyan styling (`renderer/styles-control-room.css:498-524`).
- The post-fix desktop expanded capture has only the drawer-header Close control; the redundant prompt-card Close is hidden on desktop (`renderer/styles-control-room.css:3996-3998`). On mobile, Collapse, Copy, and Close remain the focused top row.
- Minor polish: the narrow header is dense, and the mobile Close action's rose styling is stronger than its non-destructive meaning warrants.

### 3. Color and contrast — 9.95/10

- The final isolated readability run passes all 15 views with a minimum measured contrast of **4.70:1**.
- Per-view minima include home 5.57:1, active/waiting 6.82:1, runtime 5.43:1, terminal and long/slash composer states 4.70:1, tmux 4.92:1, settings 8.21:1, run modal 5.51:1, conversation/detail 6.30:1, tmux create 5.61:1, and reset 8.46:1.
- Cyan remains selection/navigation emphasis, green indicates live/success, amber indicates attention, and rose is reserved primarily for destructive or failure semantics.
- Disabled Send states are visibly subdued while enabled Send remains high-contrast and prominent.

### 4. Typography — 9.98/10

- The final 15-view audit reports zero text below the 12px floor.
- Long Korean and English prompt content wraps safely at normal reading sizes, including punctuation, URLs, and code-like tokens.
- User and assistant content retain a clear hierarchy through size, weight, and line-height rather than excessive color variation.
- The mobile reader's short labels preserve legibility and keep the action row from wrapping.

### 5. Spacing and responsiveness — 9.94/10

- The final audit reports zero undersized targets, overlaps, crowded control groups, or horizontal overflow across all 15 views.
- The mobile expanded reader uses a three-column single-row action grid and keeps every action at least 44px high (`renderer/styles-control-room.css:4055-4085`). Automated checks require exactly three aligned actions, no overflow, and at least six visible content lines (`scripts/readability-visual-check.js:856-882`).
- The mobile live-project marker is again a 7×7 dot rather than a nested menu tile. It remains visually compact without sacrificing the project row's 44px interaction target.
- Desktop drawer, 390×760 conversation drawer, 360px control room, project picker, terminal long draft, and slash-menu states all remain within their viewport.

### 6. Experience design — 9.95/10

- Empty and whitespace-only conversation and terminal drafts are prevented with native disabled buttons before activation; defensive submit-time guards remain in place.
- Conversation Enter respects Shift+Enter and composition guards, and refuses empty input (`renderer/app-events-dialogs.js:534-540`).
- Reset uses an in-product confirmation, preserves history, starts with focus on Cancel, isolates the background, and restores focus on cancellation.
- Long requests retain their source text, use grapheme-safe preview truncation, support expand/collapse and copy, and switch to a focused mobile reader without losing the required actions.
- The live indicator exposes `role="status"` and a localized accessible label while its decorative dot and compact hidden label stay out of the accessibility tree (`renderer/app-dashboard.js:217-224`).
- Product-specific unit/regression coverage and the hardened full interaction suite are green. The remaining minor deduction reflects the narrow-header density and strong non-destructive Close treatment noted above.
